The Solana memecoin market cooled materially this week. SOL is near $118, Bitcoin is around $83,400, and Solana memecoins collectively sit near a $3.67B market cap—roughly 8% below last Monday’s level and down 8.8% over the past 24 hours. Solana DEX volume fell about 10% week over week, while perpetual-futures volume dropped nearly 40%.

The important counterpoint: Solana’s stablecoin supply expanded 5.4% to $16.43B, and US spot Solana ETFs recorded their strongest week of inflows to date. Capital has entered the ecosystem, but traders are deploying it more selectively. This is a market of concentrated attention, short rotations and a few persistent leaders—not broad risk-on participation. 

*Market data below was captured September 28 and can move quickly.*

## Market Snapshot

- **SOL:** $118.26; down 3.7% over 24 hours but up 0.4% over seven days. 
- **Bitcoin:** $83,371; down 3.5% over seven days. 
- **Solana memecoin market cap:** $3.67B; down 8.8% over 24 hours. 
- **Solana memecoin volume:** $953M over 24 hours. 
- **Solana DEX volume:** $1.98B over 24 hours and approximately $18.26B over seven days, down 10% week over week. 
- **Solana perpetual volume:** $738M over 24 hours; weekly activity down 39.7%. 
- **Solana stablecoins:** $16.43B, up $836M over seven days. USDT supply rose almost 26%, while USDC increased 2.7%. 
- **US spot Solana ETFs:** A record $188M of net inflows during September 21–25, with all seven tracked funds positive.  

**Interpretation:** Risk appetite has narrowed rather than disappeared. Leverage and turnover are falling, but the stablecoin increase leaves meaningful dry powder available if BTC and SOL stabilize.

## Launchpads, DEXs and Capital Flows

Pump strengthened its position during the pullback:

-  Pump recorded approximately **$964M of launchpad spot volume**, **$13.25M in fees** and **$9.65M in protocol revenue** over seven days. 
-  Daily active wallets increased from approximately **155,000 on September 21 to 195,000 on September 27**. 
-  At least **250,000 tokens launched** during the last seven complete days, alongside **7,839 recorded graduations**. Those figures are activity counts, not a cohort survival rate. 
-  PumpSwap remained Solana’s largest DEX by seven-day volume at roughly **$2.67B**, narrowly ahead of Raydium at **$2.47B** and Orca at **$2.38B**.  

Competition remains meaningful, but its momentum was uneven:

- **Raydium LaunchLab:** $127.8M in seven-day spot volume. Daily launches fell from 9,985 on September 21 to 1,980 on September 27—an 80% decline. 
- **StonkFun:** Approximately $309.7M in seven-day DEX volume and $8.55M in fees. Its platform token, STONK, is down about 36% from its September 21 high. 
- **LetsBONK:** $3.14M in seven-day fees, but only 313 launches during the last seven complete days. Daily fees fell roughly 70% between September 21 and September 27.  

**Why it matters:** The market is consolidating around venues that already possess distribution and active traders. Custom-pair and creator-fee experiments remain viable, but launching a differentiated mechanic is no longer enough. Projects need retained liquidity, repeat participation and persistent attention after their initial event.

## Trading and Distribution Trends

Axiom processed approximately **$744M of Solana DEX volume** over seven days and $151M over the latest 24 hours, generating around $8.8M in Solana fees. Discovery feeds, wallet tracking and fast execution therefore continue to capture substantial value even as broader speculative activity contracts. 

The behavioral consequence is a faster, more reflexive market:

-  The same tokens reach many traders simultaneously. 
-  Early strength attracts copy flows rapidly. 
-  When growth stalls, liquidity can leave just as quickly. 
-  High volume alone is becoming less informative than holder retention, liquidity stability and continued activity after the first 48–72 hours. 

## Structural and Manipulation Risks

A new study published September 23 identified approximately **28 million sandwich attacks on Solana involving transactions intended to receive protected order flow**. The researchers found exposure at both validator and application layers. The practical lesson is that an interface displaying “MEV protection” should not be treated as a complete guarantee; route quality, slippage settings and priority handling still matter. 

A separate large-scale study of roughly 15 million pump.fun tokens documented wash trading, creator obfuscation, coordinated selling, copycat launches and social manipulation. Current Pump tracking also classified only 8.6% of sampled token volume as organic, although that model does **not** prove that all remaining activity was manipulated. 

For new launches, the most useful filters remain independently funded wallets, bundle concentration, developer holdings, locked liquidity, holder retention and whether volume survives after the initial social push.

## Emerging Coins

Inclusion reflects market structure and persistence, not a recommendation.

### $PAID — Creator-fee leader persists

**Verified data:** Approximately **$22.5M market cap**, **$1.02M primary-pool liquidity**, **$6.2M primary-pool daily volume**, **41,700 holders**, and **13 days old**. Primary venue is PumpSwap; approximately 99.9% of pool liquidity is reported locked. HTX listed PAID on September 27. 

**What changed:** Since last Monday, market cap increased from roughly $13.5M, liquidity from $656,000 and holders from 33,400. The holder count rose about 25%, while liquidity increased roughly 55%. The move therefore extended beyond a single price spike.

**Strength:** The deepest liquidity and broadest holder base among this week’s emerging set, reinforced by a recognizable creator-payment narrative and new centralized-exchange distribution.

**Weakness:** It is no longer undiscovered, and approximately 8.5% of supply was acquired through bundled buys. Expectations are now higher.

**Next seven days:**

- **Confirmation:** Market cap holds above $20M; primary liquidity remains above $900,000; holders stay above 40,000 and continue rising; creator-payment activity remains visible. 
- **Invalidation:** Liquidity falls below $750,000, market cap loses $15M, or activity disappears once listing attention fades. 

### $e/acc — Fastest extension of the fee-routing narrative

**Verified data:** Approximately **$8.2M–$9.2M market cap**, more than **$500,000 in the largest visible PumpSwap pool**, roughly **21,000 holders**, and only **two days old**. Aggregate tracked volume was approximately **$28M**. Fees are routed to the account associated with the project’s Effective Accelerationism narrative. 

**What changed:** e/acc compressed the PAID-style creator-fee concept into one of the week’s fastest holder-acquisition events. Attention came from the combination of a recognizable crypto-native narrative, direct fee attribution and strong initial turnover.

**Strength:** Rapid holder formation and liquidity spread across multiple active pools.

**Weakness:** It remains extremely young. Approximately 23% of buys were identified as bundled, and liquidity in one examined pool was not locked.

**Next seven days:**

- **Confirmation:** It survives through days three to seven with primary liquidity above $400,000, holders above 25,000 and market cap holding roughly $5M–$8M after volume normalizes. 
- **Invalidation:** Market cap loses $4M, primary liquidity falls below $250,000, or holder growth stops while turnover stays abnormally high. 

### $SI — Super Inu survives its first full week

**Verified data:** Approximately **$21.8M market cap**, **$630,000 liquidity**, **$3.5M daily volume** and **20,700 holders** at seven days old. Its primary pool is an SI/NVDAx custom pair on Raydium. MEXC listed SI on September 26. 

**What changed:** SI persisted beyond launch week and retained substantial liquidity through a weak broader market. The custom-pair structure and exchange listing expanded its distribution.

**Strength:** Meaningful liquidity and holder depth after a complete week, rather than only a launch-day print.

**Weakness:** The NVDAx quote asset adds another source of volatility. The recent exchange listing may also have pulled forward demand.

**Next seven days:**

- **Confirmation:** Market cap remains above $18M, liquidity above $500,000 and holders above 20,000 after the listing impulse. 
- **Invalidation:** Market cap falls below $15M alongside liquidity below $350,000, or custom-pair activity collapses. 

### $CALI — Growth accompanied by unusually high concentration

**Verified data:** Approximately **$4.0M market cap**, **$232,000 liquidity**, **$1.76M daily volume**, **6,400 holders**, and **11 days old** on PumpSwap. Liquidity is reported fully locked, but the developer controls more than 10% and approximately 79% of buys were classified as bundled. The token fell about 36% over the latest 24 hours. 

**What changed:** Despite the current reversal, market cap has roughly tripled from last Monday and the holder count has more than doubled. It has survived longer than most launches from its cohort.

**Strength:** Real holder growth and continued turnover beyond the initial launch window.

**Weakness:** Developer concentration and the bundle percentage create severe distribution and coordinated-selling risk.

**Next seven days:**

- **Confirmation:** Reclaims $5M with liquidity above $250,000 and continued holder growth that is not dominated by linked wallets. 
- **Invalidation:** Market cap falls below $2.5M, liquidity drops under $175,000, or bundle-associated wallets begin distributing materially. 

### $JEANPHIL — A recovery worth monitoring

**Verified data:** Approximately **$3.3M market cap**, **$258,000 liquidity**, **$1.0M daily volume**, **17,100 holders**, and **nine days old** on PumpSwap. About 99.9% of liquidity is reported locked, with only 1.4% of buys classified as bundled. MEXC listed the token on September 23. 

**What changed:** JEANPHIL was in a severe reversal last Monday but has since doubled its market cap, increased liquidity by roughly 64% and added holders while volume normalized. That is stronger evidence of persistence than its original breakout.

**Strength:** Improved liquidity and holder count after surviving a failed first impulse, with relatively low detected bundle activity.

**Weakness:** It remains a pure social meme with no durable catalyst yet. Daily volume has fallen substantially from launch-week levels.

**Next seven days:**

- **Confirmation:** Holds $2.5M–$3M, keeps liquidity above $225,000, retains more than 17,000 holders and sustains at least $750,000 in daily volume. 
- **Invalidation:** Market cap loses $2M, liquidity drops below $175,000, or turnover evaporates. 

### A useful failure case: $CLIP

CLIP has fallen from roughly **$1.1M market cap and $117,000 liquidity last Monday** to approximately **$86,000 market cap and $36,000 liquidity**. The token is now too thin for inclusion among the monitored emerging candidates.

Its decline is a reminder that novel fee mechanics can attract launch-week attention without creating durable demand.

- **Thursday:** Initial jobless claims and ISM Manufacturing.

These releases can change rate expectations and therefore crypto liquidity quickly. No major Solana-native mainnet catalyst is confirmed for the week. Alpenglow reached another public test environment, but there is still no announced mainnet date. 

### Informed expectations

1. **Selective trading remains the base case.**
    Confirmation would be SOL reclaiming $122–$125, Bitcoin holding above $85,000–$87,250 and Solana meme capitalization returning toward $4B. A SOL loss of $112–$115 or meme capitalization below $3.5B would reinforce risk-off conditions. 
2. **Creator-fee projects should separate into a few leaders and many failed copies.**
    Continued liquidity and holder growth in PAID and e/acc would confirm the narrative. Simultaneous liquidity deterioration across the leaders would indicate that the theme has exhausted itself. 
3. **Custom-pair momentum needs renewed launch activity.**
    A LaunchLab recovery above roughly 5,000 daily launches and a StonkFun volume rebound would support another rotation. Continued activity near current levels would keep the opportunity concentrated in existing survivors. 
4. **Lower leverage could produce healthier spot conditions—or simply reflect weak demand.**
    Rising spot DEX volume while perpetual activity stays contained would be constructive. Continued declines in both would indicate capital is remaining idle despite higher stablecoin balances.

SLC WEEKLY MARKET BRIEF — PUBLISHING GUIDE

The Market Brief system is already built into the website.

EVERY MONDAY:

1. Open:
   src/content/market-briefs/

2. Copy this file:
   _TEMPLATE.md

3. Rename the COPY to that Monday's date, for example:
   2026-09-21.md

   IMPORTANT: Do not leave the underscore at the beginning. Files beginning
   with _ are templates and are intentionally hidden from the live website.

4. Open your new .md file with Notepad or your code editor.

5. Update the five lines at the top:
   title
   date
   slug
   issue
   excerpt

6. Paste the week's brief underneath the second --- line.

7. Save.

8. Open GitHub Desktop.
   - Summary example: Add September 21 market brief
   - Commit to main
   - Push origin

Cloudflare will rebuild the site automatically.

The website automatically:
- sorts the newest brief first
- updates the homepage with the latest brief
- adds the brief to the archive
- gives it a URL at /market-briefs/YYYY-MM-DD

DO NOT edit App.jsx each Monday. Only add a new .md file.
